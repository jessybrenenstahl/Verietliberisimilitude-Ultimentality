# Two-Layer Separation

**Two-layer separation distinguishes the system-level form of a corruption from the role-level failures involved in producing it.** The three Theodicytes classify term-absolutization; the five SPLCW roles describe functions of participation. Relating the two can explain how a failure develops and where correction can act.

## The two layers

**Layer A — the three system-level [[theodicytes|Theodicytes]].** Each is defined by which non-aim *term* over-dominates, read at the level of self, world, and medium: [[spectre|the Spectre]] (world [[absolutization|absolutized]]), [[nephilim|the Nephilim]] (self absolutized), and [[homunculus|the Homunculus]] (medium absolutized). These are large-scale, system-level pathologies — the shape a whole participant's failure takes.

**Layer B — the five [[splcw|SPLCW]] role-captures.** Inside the meaning-processing cycle, each of five roles has its own characteristic failure, one per role: [[warden|the Warden]] → rigidity; [[captive|the Captive]] → collapse and confusion; [[logician|the Logician]] → sterile flattening; [[poet|the Poet]] → decorative drift; [[sculptor|the Sculptor]] → premature action. These are fine-grained, internal — failures of the individual faculties that together carry out an act of meaning.

The two layers are *complementary*, not a relabeling of one another. They describe two genuinely different registers of failure — the system-level shape and the role-level mechanism — and seeing both is richer than seeing either alone.

## Why the relation is not an identity

Five roles cannot correspond one-to-one with three Theodicytes, but five items can certainly be partitioned into three unequal groups. Arithmetic therefore does not establish that a partition is inappropriate. The reason for retaining affinities is functional: one role can contribute to different system-level failures, and several roles can contribute to the same failure.

For example, [[logician|Logician]] can contribute to [[nephilim|Nephilim]] when reasoning protects self-authorizing judgment, or to [[homunculus|Homunculus]] when formal correctness displaces living use. These are different failure mechanisms of a capacity whose healthy function remains valuable.

[[role-corruption-affinities|Role–Corruption Affinities]] gives the familiar coalitions and explains their context. A useful mapping identifies the behavior connecting the levels. It need not force every case into one exclusive assignment, and its edges need not all be equally strong.

## Using both levels for repair

The system-level description identifies what has taken the whole's place. The role-level description identifies where participation is impaired. Restoring a boundary, improving discrimination, making a symbol answer its subject, or reconnecting action to returned consequences may address different parts of the same pattern. The appropriate intervention follows the actual mechanism.

## Common misreadings

- **"It's the same list told twice."** No. The layers are complementary registers, not a relabeling; collapsing Layer B onto Layer A is the original mistake.
- **"Each role belongs to one corruption."** No. Role → Theodicyte is a coalition (soft affinity), never an identity or partition.
- **"The mapping is fixed."** No. The authority corrected the mapping over time, marking it a revisable affinity rather than a settled derivation.
- **"A different mapping is automatically a contradiction."** Different edges can describe different mechanisms; incompatible claims about the same mechanism still require examination.

## Formal status

> **Epistemic (E):** [[derived|Derived]]. The two-layer distinction and the coalition relation are held at the [[authority-canonical-tier|authority-canonical tier (AC)]]; the specific role → Theodicyte edges are [[derived|Derived]] [[carving-tier|carving-tier (CV)]] soft affinities, contestable by counter-instance.
> **Alethic (A):** The two layers aspire to map two genuinely distinct failure registers, and the functional overlap explains why exclusive role assignments would lose relevant relations.
> **Provenance:** the two-layer distinction and the coalition relation are flagged authority-canonical (canonical); the specific edges are carving-tier soft affinities, and the correction against forced partition is the edition's own (treatise-side).
