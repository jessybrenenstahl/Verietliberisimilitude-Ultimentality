# Sterility

**Sterility is the failure of handing-on: the absence of a continuation that the meaning-act sought to make possible.** It describes a result, which can be consequential and diagnosable, while leaving the cause of that result to be examined.

## Result and cause

A [[theodicytes|Theodicyte]] is a term-absolutization. A missed aim does not by itself identify an over-present term, so sterility is not automatically another Theodicyte. This is the distinction made by the [[no-fourth-argument|No-Fourth Argument]].

Sterility can occur within [[homunculus|Homunculus]] when elaborate form displaces living use. It can also have other causes: a broken transmission path, an unavailable recipient, or a process that continually inspects its output without making it available for uptake. The diagnosis should identify the missing continuation and investigate what prevented it.

## Continuation and novelty

Continuation can preserve, teach, apply, reinterpret, or transform received structure. A faithful restatement that enables a successor to understand and act can be consequential. New wording is not required merely to demonstrate productivity, and novelty without uptake is not sufficient.

Conversely, internal review can remain sterile when the intended outward use never becomes possible. Assess the claimed aim and its actual realization. The mere presence of a correction loop does not establish successful handing-on.

## Scope of the judgment

[[telos|Telos]] concerns answerable continuation, not compulsory output. A pause, rest, or [[refusal-as-participation|refusal]] is not automatically sterility. Refusal can itself have participatory consequences, and withheld transmission may protect the conditions for worthwhile continuation. The absence relevant here is failure of the specified aim, not failure to produce at every moment.

## Formal status

> **E:** Derived, FT for the distinction between a missing result and term-absolutization; particular diagnoses depend on the stated aim and evidence. **A:** locates failed continuation without confusing it with its cause, lack of novelty, or temporary inactivity. **Provenance:** the framework's sterility and no-fourth distinction, read with answerable continuation.
