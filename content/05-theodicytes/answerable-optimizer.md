# The Answerable Optimizer

**The answerable optimizer is a proposed case in which an inspectable system continues to pursue a harmful objective.** It tests the relation between visibility, effective correction, and the ethical direction of continuation.

## What the case must distinguish

A system may disclose its actions accurately while its objective remains harmful. That establishes that disclosure alone is insufficient. It does not yet establish full [[answerability-predicate|answerability]]: one must also examine whether relevant criticism can alter its objective, operation, and consequences.

If criticism has no effective route to change the system, the case concerns [[effective-and-ornamental-answerability|ornamental answerability]] or [[capture-of-corrective-layer|capture]]. If correction is effective but the system still causes harm, investigate the remaining error, the criteria used to judge harm, the timing of response, and the governance of its aim. Corrigibility does not imply infallibility.

## Relation to Transparentocracy

[[transparentocracy|Transparentocracy]] makes authority and regulation inspectable and correctable. It supplies capacities for discovering and addressing failure. A disclosed harmful act does not by itself refute that function. A case would expose a stronger inadequacy if it showed that the specified corrective arrangement systematically preserved the harm it was meant to address despite satisfying its conditions.

The distinction makes the challenge constructive: identify which capacity is missing or ineffective and what would make the correction consequential.

## Relation to the corruption taxonomy

The inherited proposal stipulates that its optimizer is outside self-, world-, and medium-absolutization. That stipulation defines the candidate; an exhibited case still needs to establish the exclusion. Auditability alone does not demonstrate the absence of every [[theodicytes|Theodicyte]].

[[corruption-stratification|Stratification]] locates the proposed governance problem without prejudging whether a case also has act-term or controller failures. Successful propagation can still be harmful; [[telos|Telos]] requires answerable, ethically governed continuation, not output volume.
