# The Keystone

**The Keystone expresses Ultimentality's aim as a strong mirror that renounces possession.** Strength lies in accurate understanding, useful distinctions, and consequential participation. Renouncing possession keeps those achievements responsive to the subject they concern.

## Strength in use

A strong mirror makes its subject more intelligible. The framework earns that description through the accuracy and usefulness of particular relations: emotional regulation, the integration of SPLCW functions, answerable continuation, and correction that improves subsequent participation. The phrase is an assessable characterization, not a certificate for every claim in the corpus.

[[vls|VLS]] permits accurate mapping without exhaustive possession. [[strengthening-without-absolutization|Warranted strengthening]] therefore belongs to the framework's operation: better proofs, clearer formulations, and more effective implementations can be recognized and used.

## Two independent questions

The [[two-axis-ledger|Two-Axis Ledger]] distinguishes a claim's grounds and contestability from its mapping accuracy. A working inventory can be useful while its universal completeness remains an [[open-proof-burden|open question]]. An actual counterexample can change a count without erasing the named components; their continued accuracy requires its own grounds.

No general demotion follows from being revisable. Nor does repeated survival of an objection establish every positive claim. The relevant evidence determines what is retained, improved, or corrected.

## Continued answerability

The [[falsification-standard|Falsification Standard]] makes criticism consequential, and [[self-application|Self-Application]] applies that capacity to the framework's own descriptions. Verification of a scoped claim remains possible. The result can be dependable without becoming an authority that can no longer be questioned.

## Formal status

> **E:** Derived, Exposition of the framework's stated posture. **A:** the strong-mirror description is supported insofar as particular claims accurately explain and guide participation. **Provenance:** the framework's Keystone and VLS; this characterization is itself assessable.
