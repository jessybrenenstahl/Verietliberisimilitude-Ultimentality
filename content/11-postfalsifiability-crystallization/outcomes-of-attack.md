# The Outcomes of Attack

## Definition

An attack record distinguishes **what examination established** from **what changed in response**. A sound objection can expose an error before the target is repaired. A test can also support a claim, reveal a functional dependency, remain inconclusive, or fail to address its target.

The framework retains four useful dispositions:

1. **Deletion:** remove a defeated claim and repair its dependencies.
2. **Revision:** change wording, scope, dependency, formulation, or governed behavior.
3. **Status change or retyping:** change the claim's warranted classification. This can include demotion or a stronger status supported by a new argument; neither is licensed by mere survival.
4. **Invariant recognition:** a specified removal test establishes a functional loss under the tested conditions. Record the dependency and its scope.

These are overlapping reporting categories, not a proven exhaustive and disjoint partition. Retyping is a kind of revision; invariant recognition concerns evidence about a relation and need not change that relation. Multiple dispositions can apply to one examination.

## Supporting evidence without exemption

A failed objection by itself does not establish its target's truth. Distinguish an irrelevant objection or inconclusive probe from a discriminating test whose successful result supports the claim tested. Successful prediction, reconstruction, intervention, and demonstrated functional dependence can strengthen a warrant. None makes a claim immune to further evidence.

An invariant-recognition result supports the named dependency under the removal conditions. It does not prove that every alternative arrangement would fail or that the present formulation is uniquely possible.

## Type and formal status

**E: Derived, CV.** The reporting scheme is revisable. Its usefulness rests on distinguishing evidence, disposition, and remaining uncertainty, not on protecting a count of four.

**A:** The scheme aims to describe actual examination and correction faithfully. It must accommodate both demonstrated failures and successful evidence without inventing either.

## Recording a resolution

```yaml
target: exact claim and scope
attack_type: kind of examination
evidence: observations or argument, with source
result: refuted / supported-within-scope / inconclusive / failed-objection
outcome: dispositions taken, or none
changed_content: actual changes, or none
changed_marks: warranted status changes, or none
changed_links: affected dependencies, or none
remaining_kill_condition: what would challenge the surviving claim
```

The fields are a proposed audit format. Missing fields make a record incomplete; they do not erase an actual resolution. A filled form does not prove that the described event occurred. Link the record to inspectable evidence and distinguish proposed work from completed changes.

## What it regulates

The scheme prevents mere persistence from becoming proof, and prevents a justified retention from being mistaken for failure to learn. It also distinguishes a refutation from its implementation: an unrepaired error can be refuted and still remain in the governed system.

## What regulates it

[[causal-falsifiability|Causal falsifiability]] checks whether a warranted correction can affect the target. [[same-level-attack-rule|Same-level examination]] and [[attack-type-matching|attack-type matching]] connect evidence to the actual claim; they do not dismiss concrete evidence solely because it concerns an implementation of an abstract function. [[two-mark-system|The two marks]] keep warranted status distinct from accuracy. [[scar-record|The scar record]] preserves reasons and evidence for retained constraints.

## Valid attack surface

Show that the scheme cannot represent a relevant result, confuses evidence with action, or permits an unsupported necessity claim to be recorded as a demonstrated dependency. Overlap is not itself a defect in explicitly overlapping categories; ambiguity that prevents useful reporting is.

## What happens if isolated

Labels without evidence can misreport correction. Evidence without an effective update path can leave a known error in operation. A prohibition on all supporting evidence makes the account unable to recognize successful tests. Inspect which of these failures actually occurs.

## What larger property emerges from the coupling

The result is an auditable correction record: defeats change what they should, successful tests supply their warranted support, unresolved questions remain identifiable, and surviving claims stay correctable.

## What would actually kill the claim

The reporting scheme requires revision if it cannot distinguish failed objections, supported results, demonstrated dependencies, and implemented corrections in cases where those differences matter. A better scheme can replace its categories while preserving this function.

## Prohibited misreadings

- **Support as exemption:** stronger evidence does not close a claim to correction.
- **No change as no result:** justified retention or a demonstrated dependency can be informative.
- **A complete form as proof:** verify the evidence it cites.
- **A missing record as a missing event:** distinguish uncertainty about history from disproof of it.
