# The Self-Sealing Test

## Definition

The **self-sealing test** examines whether relevant evidence can still change a claim or its governed operation. Self-sealing occurs where the correction path is closed, for example when every substantive objection is recast as support regardless of what it shows.

The test assesses answerability, not the truth of every proposition examined. A valid refutation remains valid even if the target refuses to update. Conversely, a justified decision to retain a claim is not evidence of sealing.

## Procedure

For each relevant question, name the target, the evidence that would warrant change, the available update path, and the evidence for the verdict:

- Can a central term be revised or deleted when warranted?
- Can a count change on adequate grounds?
- Can a mark or status change in the warranted direction?
- Can an attributed authority or its scope be corrected?
- Can a corrective process change or inhibit the relevant action?
- Can an objection be preserved and evaluated without compulsory reinterpretation as support?
- Can a demonstrated defect in this test lead to its repair or replacement?

The questions are probes, not a demand that every process possess every kind of authority or intervention. A system without a governing authority need not invent one to replace. A correction can operate internally. Record a justified “not applicable” where a probe has no target.

```text
pass           — evidence establishes the relevant correction capacity
partial        — some necessary route or effect is available, with a stated limitation
fail           — evidence establishes a relevant blocked correction path
untested       — the capacity has not been assessed
inconclusive   — assessment does not settle the capacity
not applicable — the probe has no relevant target, with reason
```

Review after material changes to the correction apparatus and where evidence suggests sealing. This page supplies a review procedure; it does not claim an executable test suite or a completed audit of every wiki page.

## Type and formal status

**E: Derived, CV.** The questions and verdicts are revisable diagnostic choices.

**A:** The procedure aims to detect actual open and closed correction paths at the stated scope. False passes and false failures are errors of the instrument or its application.

## What it regulates

It checks [[postfalsifiability|correction histories]] and [[crystallization|stable formulations]] for immunity falsely presented as achievement. It also prevents a diagnosis of sealing from becoming immune to evidence that the target does correct itself. [[outcomes-of-attack|Successful tests]] can support a verdict without making that verdict final.

## What regulates it

[[self-application|Self-application]] requires applying the same evidential standard to this test. Declaring the checklist revisable is not a demonstrated pass. Cite an actual correction, an inspected effective pathway, or other adequate evidence; otherwise leave the appropriate item untested or inconclusive.

[[causal-falsifiability|Causal uptake]] distinguishes a diagnostic's accuracy from whether the governed system acts on it. A correct diagnosis can be ignored. That establishes an implementation failure where uptake was claimed, not automatic falsity of the diagnosis.

[[same-level-attack-rule|Scope-matched examination]] keeps the target explicit. [[strengthening-without-absolutization|Strengthening]] permits warranted positive findings and repairs. [[semantic-closure-and-recursive-marking|Closure and marking]] keep the test itself assessable; an external participant can supply evidence without becoming an extra-symbolic or infallible tribunal.

## Valid attack surface

Show that a probe falsely passes a sealed system, falsely fails an answerable one, misses a relevant mechanism, or conflates untested with established capacity. Challenge an asserted implementation by showing that a finding cannot reach the relevant operation.

## What happens if isolated

Verdicts without evidence become labels. Accurate findings without an update path may fail to improve the target. Requiring endless certification of auditors can prevent action; the presence of another auditor alone does not create that regress.

## What larger property emerges from the coupling

The procedure supports auditable openness: identified correction paths, demonstrated repairs, and explicit limits. A successful repair is a positive result. It can finish while the test and the repaired system remain open to relevant new evidence.

## What would actually kill the claim

Revise the instrument where it systematically misclassifies relevant cases or cannot distinguish effective correction from sealing. Repair its integration where sound findings cannot produce the claimed effects. Do not merge these two verdicts.

## Prohibited misreadings

- **Pass as universal correctness:** the verdict concerns a specified correction capacity.
- **Declarations as completed tests:** inspect the path and record supporting evidence.
- **Unchanged content as sealing:** establish that warranted correction is blocked.
- **Demotion as the only healthy outcome:** status follows evidence in either direction.
- **Refusal to obey as diagnostic disproof:** validity and uptake are distinct.
