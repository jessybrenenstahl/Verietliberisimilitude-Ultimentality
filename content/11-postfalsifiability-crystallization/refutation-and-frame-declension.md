# Refutation and Frame Declension

## Definition

**Refutation** establishes that a claim fails under the conditions it asserts. **Frame declension** declines some or all of the vocabulary, aims, assumptions, or commitments used to frame it. These are distinguishable moves, but they need not be mutually exclusive or exhaust every form of criticism.

A critic can provisionally use a frame's assumptions to demonstrate an internal contradiction without adopting its purposes. A critic can also reject a premise on evidence, challenge an application, or propose a better framework. Identify the particular move and what it establishes.

## Type and formal status

**E: Derived, CV.** The distinction is a revisable method for describing disputes. It does not divide FT claims into an immune class and CV claims into a refutable class. A purported FT derivation remains open to internal examination.

**A:** The distinction aims to track whether an argument shows failure of an asserted commitment, declines that commitment, or does both. Ambiguous cases can remain unresolved without erasing clear cases.

## What it regulates

A maintainer must not relabel an internal contradiction as mere refusal of the vocabulary. Equally, declining a stipulation does not by itself refute a valid inference conditional on that stipulation. Neither move constitutes the critic's defeat.

## What regulates it

[[same-level-attack-rule|Relevance]] locates the exact claim and [[attack-type-matching|matching]] identifies its commitments. [[self-sealing-test|Self-sealing review]] keeps these classifications appealable. [[causal-falsifiability|Causal uptake]] distinguishes the argument's validity from whether the target changes: an ignored refutation is not thereby a failed argument.

## Comparing alternatives

Where a comparison is claimed, examine the explanatory and operational capacities retained, lost, or gained. State the criteria and evidence. Declining a vocabulary does not automatically lose its functions; another account may preserve or improve them.

The framework can ask what follows from an alternative without making permission to leave depend on paying a compulsory “cost.” [[refusal-as-participation|Refusal has consequences]]; that fact alone does not establish a net loss or require the decliner to adopt the framework's valuation. A person can also decline further participation without proposing a replacement theory.

## Valid attack surface

Show that the distinction misclassifies an argument, cannot represent a mixed challenge, or conceals a premise dispute as an obligation to accept the frame. Challenge a claimed cost by testing whether the alleged lost capacity is actually unavailable under the alternative.

## What happens if isolated

Treating only internal refutation as legitimate can restrict discussion to one vocabulary. Treating every objection as declension can shield internal errors. Identify the actual exclusion or evasion rather than inferring it from the absence of a label.

## What larger property emerges from the coupling

The distinction enables honest criticism across and within frameworks. It allows the framework to defend a valid conditional inference, repair an invalid one, and compare alternatives without mistaking departure for defeat or charging an unsupported price for it.

## What would actually kill the claim

Revise the method where it systematically misclassifies disputes or protects internal errors from examination. An unmeasurable cross-frame comparison limits that comparison; it does not make refutation and refusal indistinguishable in every case.

## Prohibited misreadings

- **Declension as the only challenge to FT:** test the stated inference and application.
- **Refutation as adopting all purposes:** premises can be assumed for argument.
- **Declension as automatic lost capacity:** compare what the alternative can do.
- **No uptake as no refutation:** truth and correction implementation are separate.
- **Two distinct moves as an exhaustive binary:** mixed and partial challenges are possible.
