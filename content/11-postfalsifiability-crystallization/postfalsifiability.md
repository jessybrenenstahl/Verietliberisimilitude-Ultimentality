# Postfalsifiability

## Definition

**Postfalsifiability** names a correction architecture that carries the consequences of earlier testing forward into its present structure while keeping that structure answerable. A demonstrated error leads to a repair; the reason for the repair remains available so compression does not silently restore the error.

This adds a specific emphasis to falsifiability: retained consequences and inspectable correction history. Ordinary falsifiability does not forbid memory, repeated testing, or improvement. The two need not be rival, mutually exclusive categories.

```text
examination → evidence → warranted disposition
implemented correction → retained reason and appropriate check
current structure → continued exposure to relevant criticism
```

A present relation may be defined, derived, newly proposed, supported by successful tests, or revised after failure. Do not invent a history of repeated defeat for every relation. State which history and evidence actually support the claim being made.

## Type and formal status

**E: Derived, CV.** Postfalsifiability is a revisable account of how correction can accumulate. It introduces no exempt tier.

**A:** Applied to a particular corpus, it makes a historical and functional claim: relevant corrections occurred, affected the current structure, and remain effective. Its definition alone cannot establish that history; records, source changes, arguments, and tests can supply the evidence.

## What it regulates

The account bounds loss of the reasons for a correction and the promotion of survival into immunity. It supports accumulated achievement: repaired errors need not be repeatedly rediscovered, and successful tests can strengthen warranted confidence.

A correction episode or a particular distillation can finish. The resulting structure remains correctable. Development can have stages; ongoing capacity does not erase chronology or require perpetual active revision.

## What regulates it

- [[causal-falsifiability|Causal falsifiability]] separates an established refutation from its effect on the governed target.
- [[outcomes-of-attack|Outcome records]] distinguish failed objections, supporting evidence, and implemented corrections.
- [[scar-record|Scar records]] preserve a constraint's reason and relevant provenance.
- [[kill-table|Defeat conditions]] and [[semantic-closure-and-recursive-marking|recursive marking]] preserve effective correction of current claims.

A missing historical trace leaves the attributed history unverified; evidence that no claimed change occurred contradicts that attribution. These are different findings.

## Property versus implementation

A stated defeat condition is not an executable test. An executable test is not proof of an effective update path. An effective update path is not a truth certificate. Conversely, a reasoned review can establish a philosophical correction without a software test.

Describe checks honestly: proposed, manually assessed, or executable, with the result and scope. A gap in a required implementation can undermine a claim that the property is already realized; it does not automatically refute the usefulness of the standard. [[attack-surface-matrix|The attack-surface matrix]] must report actual coverage.

## Valid attack surface

Challenge a claimed correction history with its evidence: did the earlier error occur, did the change address it, and does the retained constraint still protect the named function? Challenge the general account by showing that its proposed retention fails to support correction or misclassifies relevant cases. A simpler account may improve the formulation without erasing the capacity described.

## What happens if isolated

Survival without effective scrutiny can become an immunity narrative. Correction without retained reasons can be forgotten. Neither failure follows merely from lacking the word “postfalsifiability”; inspect the operative process.

## What larger property emerges from the coupling

The coupling supports [[crystallization|crystallization]]: a compact, functioning structure whose tested distinctions and correction pathways survive compression. The gain is usable accumulated learning, including successful work, with sufficient evidence to assess and revise it.

## What would actually kill the claim

An application fails where it asserts effective correction but the relevant update path is closed, or claims a correction history contradicted by the record. Narrow the verdict to that application. Revise the general model if its criteria systematically confuse these failures with effective retained correction.

## Prohibited misreadings

- **Past falsification as future immunity:** prior tests do not exempt current claims.
- **Mere survival as supporting evidence:** inspect what the test actually discriminated.
- **Every relation as the survivor of repeated defeat:** establish its actual grounds.
- **Completion as uncorrectability:** completed work can remain open to relevant new evidence.
- **Missing provenance as proof that nothing happened:** mark the evidential gap.
