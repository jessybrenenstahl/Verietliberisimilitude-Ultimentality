# Attack-Type Matching

## Definition

**Attack-type matching** routes criticism toward the commitments a claim actually makes. A binding claim, for example, can be examined through its actuator, regulated error, or asserted effect. The routes are examples, not exclusive permissions: a claim may expose several attack surfaces and belong to several types.

[[same-level-attack-rule|Relevance to the target]] must be established. A successful argument is assessed on its substance, not on whether its author used the table's labels. [[outcomes-of-attack|The result]] remains separate from its implementation in [[causal-falsifiability|governed correction]].

## Type and formal status

**E: Derived, Mixed.** The page organizes existing distinctions as exposition and proposes a CV classification. Its count and grouping remain revisable.

**A:** The table has an accuracy burden: whether it routes a challenge to the claim and permits relevant evidence to reach it. Calling a table navigational does not exempt its mappings from error.

## The matching table

| Claim type | Examples of relevant examination |
|---|---|
| Frame condition | Challenge premises, internal consistency, claimed entailment, or application; distinguish declining premises from refuting a conditional inference |
| Stipulation | Decline its use, identify ambiguity or inconsistent application, or challenge a claimed consequence of adopting it |
| Carving or count | Counter-instance, missing category, unnecessary split, or a better organizing account |
| Binding | Misidentified actuator, regulated error, target, or claimed effect |
| Coupled controller | Failed mutual regulation, a controlled removal test, or a counterexample to the claimed emergent function |
| Role topology | Missing function, invalid ordering or dependency, or a realization that contradicts claimed coverage |
| Authority claim | Misattribution, conflict with the source's actual statement, inapplicable scope, or challenge to the authority asserted |
| World-mapping claim | Predictive or explanatory failure within the claimed scope |
| Continuity claim | Misidentified lineage, unsupported identity criterion, or a break in the claimed continuity |
| Answerability claim | A relevant blocked update path, capture, or a misclassification of effective correction |

These rows overlap. A concrete case can test more than one commitment. Outcomes can include retention with scoped support, inconclusive examination, revision, status change, deletion, or demonstrated dependency, as specified in [[outcomes-of-attack]].

A frame-internal claim does not become empirically immune merely by being labeled FT. Distinguish the logical conditional from an assertion that its premises or conclusions apply to a case. A stipulation fixes usage; claims about that usage and its consequences remain assessable. An authority-canonical claim must accurately represent its source as well as state why the source has the attributed authority.

## What it regulates

The table prevents burdens from being silently transferred between claims. It also prevents a claim from escaping its actual burdens by switching labels during a dispute.

## What regulates it

Read the full target, not only its compressed row. [[same-level-attack-rule|The relevance procedure]] requires a reason for excluding a challenge and permits that reason to be contested. [[self-sealing-test|Self-sealing review]] checks whether the table protects claims from evidence. [[strengthening-without-absolutization|Stronger formulations]] can be adopted where their arguments warrant them.

## Valid attack surface

Show that a row misstates a claim's commitments, omits a relevant route, falsely makes routes exclusive, or conflates evidence with uptake. A useful general argument can apply to several types without making those types identical.

## What happens if isolated

A table used without examining the target can become a gatekeeping device. Missing this particular table does not make all objections equally sound; relevance can be established by other explicit reasoning.

## What larger property emerges from the coupling

The table makes criticism easier to locate and evaluate while keeping the mapping itself correctable. It supports [[postfalsifiability|retained correction]] without installing procedural immunity.

## What would actually kill the claim

The classification needs revision if it systematically misroutes relevant criticism or cannot be applied without arbitrary retyping. A different classification can preserve or improve the same function; declining these labels does not forfeit the capacity to reason about relevance.

## Prohibited misreadings

- **One type, one permissible attack:** claims can have several commitments.
- **A matched attack as an established defeat:** examination still has to establish its result.
- **An unmatched label as an invalid argument:** inspect the connection to the target.
- **A routing table without an accuracy burden:** its assignments can be wrong.
