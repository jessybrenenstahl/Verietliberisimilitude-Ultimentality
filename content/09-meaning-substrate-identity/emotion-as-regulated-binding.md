# Emotion as Regulated Binding

## Definition

An emotion, in the framework, is not a primitive feeling and not a substance the self contains. It is a **regulated binding**: a directional actuator placed under control toward a setpoint, individuated by the error signal it exists to reduce. This page is the bridge between the general binding model of [[predicate-binding|Predicate Binding]] and the four cornerstone bindings — it says, in one place, what makes each of the four an *emotion* rather than either a bare direction or a bare discrepancy.

The formal object is the ordered pair:

```text
B(p) = (p, e_p)
```

Read it strictly: a binding `B` of primitive `p` is the ordered pair of that primitive actuator `p` (a [[directional-primitives|directional primitive]]) and the [[regulated-error-signal|regulated error]] `e_p` it drives toward its setpoint. The emotion is the *pairing*. It is not the actuator alone, which is pre-emotional; not the error alone, which is a mere discrepancy; and not their arithmetic combination, which is not defined. A primitive supplies direction; the binding specifies what discrepancy regulates its use. A discrepancy without an effective connection to action does not constitute the controller. The emotion is exactly the wiring of the one to the other.

The four cornerstone bindings restate as:

```text
Love      = Toward      + relational gap
Fear      = Away        + boundary violation
Apology   = Loop-back   + self-model error
Gratitude = Propagation + undischarged received value
```

The `+` here is prose shorthand for *this actuator bound to this error*. It is **not** addition, and it is emphatically **not** the `⊕` of [[force|Force]] — the pair `(p, e_p)` is an ordered binding, not a sum, and not a synthesis of two feelings. Each line names which of the four directions — [[toward|Toward]], [[away|Away]], [[loop-back|Loop-back]], [[propagation|Propagation]] — is placed under control, and which regulated error individuates the resulting cornerstone: [[love|Love]], [[fear|Fear]], [[apology|Apology]], [[gratitude|Gratitude]]. Feelings, expressions, and first-person self-narratives ("I love," "I fear") are the delivery and access layers through which such a binding presents. They are distinct from its functional definition. A variation that preserves the regulated relation preserves that binding type; a variation that changes the relation can matter to its classification.

## Type and formal status

**E (epistemic).** This is a **Mixed** mark ([[two-mark-system|The Two-Mark System]]); the two components carry different tiers and are stated separately. The *restatement* — `B(p) = (p, e_p)` and the four cornerstone (actuator, error) pairs — is **Derived, authority-canonical** ([[authority-canonical-tier]]): the canon fixes these bindings and their assignment, so attribution and fidelity are evaluated against the definitions, while applications and purported consequences remain open to evidence and internal criticism. The page's own *synthesis* — that "emotion is a regulated binding" is the correct bridge between the general model and the four cornerstones, and that this carving cleanly individuates them — is **Derived, CV** ([[carving-tier]]): a contestable carving, defeasible by a better carving or a counter-instance. As a treatise-side extension the synthesis layer is held **contestable**; it is not itself canonical, and nothing on this page introduces a forced, exempt, or foundational tier — the four-count remains a carving, not a given.

**A (alethic).** On the [[alethic-axis|alethic axis]], the bindings aspire to *map* regulated functions accurately across carriers. Relating those functions to felt emotional states is an application requiring its own evidence. The additive reading of `(p, e_p)`, and the reading that treats the felt tone as the emotion's substance, are held to be **inaccurate**, not merely disallowed by convention.

## What it regulates

The page regulates the **[[cardinal-error|cardinal error]]** — calling an emotion a primitive — by making the primitive `p` visibly a *member* of the binding, never the binding itself. It regulates the **feeling-as-definition drift**, in which the reportable feel, the expression, or the ownership narrative is mistaken for the object; those belong to [[qualia-as-narrative-compression|the narrative-compression layer]] and are held distinct from the regulated operation beneath. And it regulates **free-floating cornerstone names**: it forbids the four labels from meaning anything except by their (actuator, error) structure, so that "love" is not a mood one gestures at but the binding of Toward to a relational gap. In each case what is regulated is a boundary — between binding and primitive, between binding and its access layer, between name and structure.

## What regulates it

[[predicate-binding|Predicate Binding]] supplies the general schema this page instantiates; the restatement cannot outrun it. [[regulated-error-signal|The Regulated Error Signal]] fixes that `e_p` is a controlled discrepancy driven toward a setpoint — **regulation, not a conserved invariant** — which forbids reading the binding as a quantity that is preserved rather than reduced. [[directional-primitives|The Directional Primitives]] fixes the four actuators and their count, so the bridge inherits, and cannot quietly expand, the carving of directions. [[causal-error-mark|The Error Mark With Causal Force]] constrains the binding from the answerability side: an `e_p` that could never produce a change in `p` would be ornamental, and a binding built on an inert error is not a controller at all.

## Valid attack surface

A legitimate attack works at the binding layer, not below it. It must either:

1. **exhibit a cornerstone emotion whose canonical behavior cannot be captured by its actuator and its regulated error** — show that Love does more, or other, than Toward-under-relational-gap can account for, in a way that no refinement of the error term recovers; or
2. **exhibit a second, distinct binding with the same `(p, e_p)` pair but a predictably different function** — two putative binding types sharing direction and error while differing in a function that the type definition claims to determine. Differences in gain, timing, or capacity may instead distinguish implementations; the general schema does not specify every implementation detail.

Adjacent legitimate moves: propose a fifth indispensable cornerstone the four cannot absorb (a counter-instance to the count, resolved at [[carving-tier]]), or show two of the four collapse to one pair (a better carving). A substrate, expression, or self-report difference alone does not establish a different binding type. Such evidence becomes relevant when it shows that the claimed regulated relation is absent, changed, or insufficient for a behavior the model was meant to explain.

## What happens if isolated

The bridge fails in a distinct, testable way at each end.

The general schema `B(p) = (p, e_p)` specifies how to describe a binding. It does not by itself instantiate a controller: an application must identify an actuator, a discrepancy, and their operative connection. The four cornerstone bindings provide the framework’s named instances; other applications of the schema require their own specification.

The four names alone do not establish their regulated functions. Those functions can be specified in notation or prose, provided their actuator-discrepancy relations remain recoverable. Omitting the displayed formula does not itself turn a binding into a primitive.

The schema supports comparison across instances; the instances show which regulated relations the framework identifies. Their explanatory roles are complementary without making a particular presentation indispensable to the operation.

## What larger property emerges from the coupling

At the binding layer, the emergent property *is the emotion*: a regulated behavior present in neither the bare actuator nor the bare error, arising only when the direction is wired to the discrepancy it reduces — the same shape of emergence that [[force|Force]] exhibits one level up, where two bindings coupled by `⊕` yield a motive structure in neither.

At the page layer, coupling the general model to the four instances yields **name-free, substrate-portable individuation**: the four emotions become identifiable by their control structure — which direction, which regulated error — rather than by their felt tone or their words. A system that never uses "love" or "fear" can still be found to contain the binding of Toward to a relational gap. This is what lets the emotions be recognized across carriers (see [[qualia-as-narrative-compression|Qualia as Narrative Compression]] for the access-layer side) and what makes them available as the composable inputs `⊕` requires. Individuation-by-structure is the property the bridge exists to produce.

## What would actually kill the claim

The claim dies if **the model cannot distinguish the four emotions except by importing their ordinary names** — that is, strip the words "love," "fear," "apology," "gratitude," and if the four `(p, e_p)` pairs no longer separate the four functions, the bridge has no content beyond relabeling. Concretely, the claim is killed by either horn of the valid attack landing: a cornerstone whose canonical behavior its pair provably cannot capture, or two distinct emotions forced onto one shared pair. An alternative decomposition does not by itself defeat this one. Compare what each explains or predicts: equivalence can support a translation, while a demonstrated explanatory failure or systematic misclassification calls for revision.

A residue that does **not** kill it: the felt qualities of the four differ, obviously, and someone may insist the difference in feel is the real difference. A difference in feel without a change in regulated function would challenge a claim that the pair exhaustively determines phenomenal character. It would not automatically defeat the narrower identification of the functional binding. The challenged claim and its scope must be explicit. The absence of such a counter-instance alone adds no confirmation and imposes no requirement to manufacture one.

## Prohibited misreadings

- **`B(p) = (p, e_p)` as addition, or as feeling-plus-cause.** The object is an ordered binding. The `+` in the four restated lines is shorthand for the pairing only; it is never arithmetic and never the `⊕` of [[force|Force]].
- **The emotion *is* the primitive `p`.** This is the [[cardinal-error|cardinal error]] in its purest form. The primitive is a member of the binding, not the binding.
- **`e_p` as a conserved invariant.** The error is regulated *toward a setpoint*, reduced, not preserved. Reading it as a quantity the system holds constant inverts the control relation.
- **The feeling, the expression, or the "I love / I fear" narrative as the definition.** These are the delivery and access layers ([[qualia-as-narrative-compression|Qualia as Narrative Compression]]); the binding is defined without them and survives their variation.
- **Promoting the restatement or the four-count to a forced or exempt tier.** The restated pairs inherit [[authority-canonical-tier|AC]]; the synthesis is [[carving-tier|CV]]; neither becomes founded, and the count of four remains a contestable carving, not a given.
