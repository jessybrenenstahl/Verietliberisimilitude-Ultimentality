# The Constitutive Identification (conatus)

**The constitutive identification connects being a meaning-bearing self with orientation toward the continuation of meaning-bearing structure.** It states the framework's account of what that form of participation involves. The corpus also names this orientation *conatus*.

## Constitutive orientation

Under this identification, continuation is part of the framework's conception of the meaning-bearing self, rather than an additional preference that must be consciously chosen on each occasion. [[telos|Telos]] articulates this orientation as [[symbolic-immortality|answerable symbolic immortality]], extending beyond the finite carrier through subsequent participation.

Orientation does not guarantee successful realization. A participant may misunderstand, obstruct, or fail its aim. The [[answerability-predicate|answerability predicate]] also distinguishes continuation worth carrying from mere persistence. These distinctions let the identification guide analysis without turning every observable outcome into success by definition.

## Definition, derivation, and application

A constitutive definition fixes a use of a term. A derivation must show that its conclusion follows from the stated premises. An application must establish that the subject being described fits those premises. These are distinct tasks.

If orientation is included in the definition of a meaning-bearing self, its attribution follows under that definition. The definition alone does not independently demonstrate that every proposed real-world instance fits it, or that every particular desire successfully realizes the Telos. Those applications can be examined through conduct, explanation, and consequences.

A reader can also expose equivocation, inconsistency, or an invalid inference while retaining the framework. Merely using its vocabulary does not defeat an objection. [[frame-internal-tier|FT]] retains internal criticism alongside the option of declining the frame.

## Strength and continued use

The identification supplies a coherent way to connect immediate participation with what it makes possible beyond the present carrier. Its strength is developed through sound relations and accurate application. [[derived|Derived]] status keeps those grounds explicit while permitting dependable use and further development.

## Formal status

> **E:** Derived, FT for the identification within its stated definition; the definition's authorized role and the accuracy of its applications are separate questions. **A:** maps orientation and continuation without conflating definition with independently established behavior. **Provenance:** the framework's constitutive identification, read with the kernel's permission for internal criticism (§0.21) and account of Telos (§8).
