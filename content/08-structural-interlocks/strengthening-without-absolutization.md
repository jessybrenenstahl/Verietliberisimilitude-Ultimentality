# Strengthening Without Absolutization

**Strengthening brings a claim, formulation, or implementation into better agreement with its grounds and purpose.** It can establish a stronger relation, clarify a sentence, improve performance, or recognize support previously overlooked. Absolutization begins where a result is treated as immune to relevant correction.

## What can improve

A proof can establish a dependency previously presented as optional. An implementation can regulate an error more effectively. A revised definition can express a distinction with fewer ambiguities. Evidence can justify greater confidence in a judgment or authority's reliability. These are real achievements; [[derived|Derived]] status does not require withholding recognition of them.

For each improvement, state what changed, what establishes the gain, and the scope for which it holds. A clearer sentence is better wording; a more capable controller is a better implementation. Neither requires a claim of final or universal perfection.

## Classification follows warrant

The [[two-mark-system|two marks]] distinguish how a claim is supported from how accurately it maps its subject. A [[carving-tier|CV]] proposal may acquire a demonstrated [[frame-internal-tier|FT]] entailment under explicit premises. This records a stronger result while leaving the derivation inspectable for internal incoherence or invalid steps.

A corrected type need not be arbitrarily reversible: once a category mistake is repaired, returning to it is not an improvement. What must remain available is response to relevant new evidence or a demonstrated error. Openness means effective correction, not an obligation to undo valid work.

## Productive stability

A well-supported result can be retained and used. Repeatedly lowering its status without counter-warrant would itself misrepresent the evidence. Conversely, a successful result in one domain does not grant every application, sentence, or source the same support.

The [[dynamic-fixed-point|Dynamic Fixed Point]] describes stability sustained through responsive operation. [[self-application|Self-Application]] keeps the account of that operation assessable. Together they permit confidence, further construction, and correction when needed.

## Evaluation

A warranted improvement should survive comparison with the previous account on the relevant task. Criticism can show that the claimed gain is absent, that it depends on changed premises, or that its scope has been overstated. It can also show that an overly restrictive evaluation rule refuses to recognize a real gain.

An [[causal-error-mark|effective error mark]] can revise the result or its classification. The [[outcomes-of-attack|outcomes of attack]] record the actual consequence. A failed objection by itself supplies no additional proof, while a successful prediction, reconstruction, or intervention can provide positive evidence for the claim it tests.
