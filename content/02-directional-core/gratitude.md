# Gratitude

In this framework, **Gratitude** is an act of passing something on, not a warm glow: it is *propagation that discharges undischarged received value* — the move of emitting outward, working to shrink the gap between value you have received and value you have carried forward. Formally it is the [[predicate-binding|predicate binding]] **Gratitude = (propagation, undischarged received value)**: the directional operator [[propagation|propagation]] driven by a controller whose target is to discharge received value into [[continuity|continuation]]. When value has been received but not yet passed on, the controller pushes to emit it; as it is carried forward, the error shrinks toward zero. Gratitude, on this account, is the *discharge*, not the feeling of being grateful.

## How it works

The three parts of the binding: the actuator is [[propagation|propagation]], the primitive that emits transformed structure into the field, another system, a medium, a trace, or a future state. The controller is named "Gratitude." The [[regulated-error-signal|regulated error signal]] is **undischarged received value** — the discrepancy between value received and value carried forward. The controller drives that discrepancy toward zero.

The framework names the contrast directly and memorably: **ingratitude hoards it; Gratitude discharges it into continuation.** This is the diagnostic. Gratitude is not measured by how warmly value is felt but by whether it is *moved onward*. The layer's accuracy discipline holds — extract the regulated error (undischarged received value), not the surface delivery channel. By analogy only, and not as doctrine: a river stays alive by flowing through, not by pooling; Gratitude is the flowing-through of received value, where ingratitude is the stagnant pool. The comparison is illustrative; the framework's claim is the control loop, not the river.

## Place on the Routing axis

Gratitude sits on the [[routing-axis|Routing axis]] — the side of a transformation step that decides where the result goes. Its actuator routes *outward*, emitting into the field or the future. Its counterpart is [[apology|Apology = (loop-back, self-model error)]], whose actuator routes *inward*, back into the self. Apology corrects the self-model; Gratitude carries value out. They are the two directions of one axis, which is what lets them couple into a single composite.

## Role in the wider framework

Gratitude carries received value onward. In [[reconciliation|Reconciliation]], it couples with [[apology|Apology]] so that inward correction supports renewed outward participation. This compositional role does not make Gratitude itself dependent on a prior fault or apology. The [[sentimental-form|sentimental form]] occurs when outward value is treated as a substitute for correction that the reconciliation actually requires.

## Common misreadings

The cardinal error: treating Gratitude as a [[directional-primitives|primitive]] rather than a binding over [[propagation|propagation]] — [[cardinal-error|the cardinal inversion]]. The characteristic error: equating Gratitude with the *feeling* of being grateful rather than the discharge of received value; hoarding while feeling grateful is ingratitude in the framework's sense. The structural error: treating outward value as sufficient Reconciliation while the relevant self-model error continues to govern conduct — the sentimental form. And the general error: reading the binding as preservation of an invariant rather than regulation toward a setpoint.
